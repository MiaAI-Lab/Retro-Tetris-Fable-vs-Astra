Fable vs Astra

Four single-file falling-block games. Open any of them — no install, no backend. Source is on GitHub under AGPL-3.0.

Fable

Graphite Blocks

Colour pencil on 5mm graph paper, photographed on 16mm. Tiles, grain, and audio are generated in the browser.

Play →

Astra

Paper Tetris

A paper arcade cabinet with rolling film grain, on-screen controls, and generated sound. Fully offline.

Play →

GLM-5.3-Flash

Sketchbook Film

Ink-and-wash sketchbook well with rolling grain, Korobeiniki chiptune, and generated tiles. Hold, next, ghost, and high score.

Play →

Qwen3.8-Flash

Pencil Tetris

Crayon-scribble tiles on a wobbly pencil grid, aged graph paper and hand-lettered scoring. Korobeiniki music, off until you ask. One file, no assets.
